\title{From the 3-particle Green's function to the Parquet equations}

\begin{abstract}
In three stages, these notes point out the connection between the Parquet and the ADC constructions of the elf-energy. First, we show that the single-particle Green's function is naturally expressed in terms of the three-particle Green's function through the Martin--Schwinger hierarchies of EOMs for $n$-order Green' functions. We then show that the channel decomposition of the three-particle vertex naturally arises from the Faddeev decomposition of the three-particle vertex. Finally, we show how the ADC of the self-energy can be derived in this way. 
\end{abstract}
