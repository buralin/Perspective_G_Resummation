\title{Third-Order Extension of the Multiresolvent Representation}

%\author{Pierre-Fran\c{c}ois \surname{Loos}}
%    \email{loos@irsamc.ups-tlse.fr}
%    \affiliation{\LCPQ}

\begin{abstract}
These companion notes extend the multiresolvent representation of the parquet
vertex to third order in the bare interaction.
For the direct electron-hole ($\eh$) vertex, the complete third-order
Bethe--Salpeter convolution is resolved into occupied--virtual ($ov$),
virtual--occupied ($vo$), occupied--occupied ($oo$), and virtual--virtual
($vv$) sectors of the non-interacting two-particle propagator.
The $ov$ and $vo$ sectors recover the usual corrections to the coupling
vertices and neutral resolvent, whereas the frequency dependence of
$\Gamma^{\eh,(2)}$ makes the $oo$ and $vv$ sectors nonzero at third order.
These latter contributions are evaluated directly as products of charged-state
resolvents, without introducing denominators involving differences between two
occupied or two virtual orbital energies.
The particle-particle ($\pp$) extension is kept preliminary here and will be
completed separately after the direct-$\eh$ construction has been audited.
\end{abstract}

\maketitle

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\section{Scope and conventions}
\label{sec:introduction}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

These notes begin where the second-order multiresolvent construction ends.
We adopt without rederivation the direct-$\eh$ frequency convention, parquet
channel definitions, normalized factors $Q^\pm$, channel metrics and
resolvents, coupling vertices $U^r$ and $V^r$, and the explicit second-order
irreducible kernels $\Gamma^{\eh,(2)}$ and $\Gamma^{\pp,(2)}$.
The second-order derivation and its independent consistency checks are not
repeated.
The objective here is solely to identify and organize the structures that
first appear at third order.

As in the second-order notes, we use a Hartree--Fock zeroth-order reference
with a M{\o}ller--Plesset partition.
Accordingly, $G_0$ and $L_0$ denote the corresponding non-interacting
one- and two-particle propagators.
All expressions are written for real-valued orbitals, and causal
prescriptions are denoted by $0^+$.

\section{Third-order multiresolvent extension}
\label{sec:multiresolvent_third}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

We take the complete second-order multiresolvent construction as given and
extend it by one perturbative order.
For either primitive channel $r=\eh,\pp$,
\begin{align}
    \ii\Phi^r
    &=
    U^rR^rV^r
    \\
    R^r(\Omega_r)
    &=
    [\Omega_r\eta^r-M^r]^{-1}
    \label{eq:third_starting_point}
\end{align}
with the frequency conventions, metrics, and lower-order quantities defined
in the second-order notes.
Expanding
\begin{align}
    U^r
    &=
    U^{r,(1)}+U^{r,(2)}+\mathcal O(v^3)
    \\
    V^r
    &=
    V^{r,(1)}+V^{r,(2)}+\mathcal O(v^3)
    \label{eq:target_UV_expansion}
    \\
    M^r
    &=
    M^{r,(0)}+M^{r,(1)}+\mathcal O(v^2)
    \label{eq:target_M_expansion}
\end{align}
gives
\begin{align}
    R^r
    &=
    R^{r,(0)}+R^{r,(1)}+\mathcal O(v^2)
    \\
    R^{r,(1)}
    &=
    R^{r,(0)}M^{r,(1)}R^{r,(0)}
    \label{eq:target_R_first}
\end{align}
and therefore the familiar third-order combination
\begin{equation}
    U^{r,(2)}R^{r,(0)}V^{r,(1)}
    +
    U^{r,(1)}R^{r,(0)}V^{r,(2)}
    +
    U^{r,(1)}R^{r,(1)}V^{r,(1)}
    \label{eq:target_third_order}
\end{equation}
This algebraic expansion is useful, but at third order it should not be taken
as the starting assumption for orbital-space completeness.
For the direct-$\eh$ vertex we therefore return to the full BSE frequency
convolution and resolve the internal non-interacting two-particle propagator
into its $ov$, $vo$, $oo$, and $vv$ orbital sectors.
The $ov$ and $vo$ terms will reproduce Eq.~\eqref{eq:target_third_order},
whereas the $oo$ and $vv$ terms must be evaluated directly.

\section{Direct eh vertex through third order}
\label{sec:eh_third}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

The direct-$\eh$ construction uses without repetition the second-order
irreducible kernel $\Gamma^{\eh,(2)}$, the normalized factors $Q^\pm$,
the zeroth-order neutral resolvent $R^{\eh,(0)}$, and the first-order
coupling vertices $U^{\eh,(1)}$ and $V^{\eh,(1)}$ established in the
second-order notes.
The purpose of this section is to evaluate every orbital sector of the
third-order frequency convolution before reorganizing the $ov/vo$ subset in
multiresolvent form.

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\subsection{Perturbative BSE expansion}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

At third order, the direct-$\eh$ reducible vertex contains three
contributions.
Suppressing the explicit internal-frequency integrations with the convolution
symbol $\circ$, and retaining the same phase convention as in the
second-order notes,
\begin{equation}
    \begin{split}
    \ii\Phi^{\eh,(3)}
    & =
    \left[\ii\Gamma^{\eh,(2)}\right]
    \circ\left[-\ii L_0^\eh\right]
    \circ\left[\ii\Gamma^{\eh,(1)}\right]
    \\
    &+
    \left[\ii\Gamma^{\eh,(1)}\right]
    \circ\left[-\ii L_0^\eh\right]
    \circ\left[\ii\Gamma^{\eh,(2)}\right]
    \\
    &+
    \left[\ii\Gamma^{\eh,(1)}\right]
    \circ\left[-\ii L_0^\eh\right]
    \circ\left[\ii\Gamma^{\eh,(1)}\right]
    \circ\left[-\ii L_0^\eh\right]
    \circ\left[\ii\Gamma^{\eh,(1)}\right]
    \end{split}
    \label{eq:Phi_eh_third_convolution}
\end{equation}
The first two terms contain the frequency-dependent second-order irreducible
kernel on opposite sides of the internal propagation.
The last term contains three instantaneous first-order interactions.

For a two-body interaction, the fully two-particle-irreducible vertex has no
second- or third-order contribution beyond the bare interaction,
\begin{equation}
    \ii\Lambda^{(1)}_{pqrs}
    =
    \mel{pq}{}{rs}
    \qquad
    \Lambda
    =
    \Lambda^{(1)}+\mathcal O(v^4)
    \label{eq:Lambda_through_third}
\end{equation}
so that Eq.~\eqref{eq:Phi_eh_third_convolution} contains the complete
third-order direct-$\eh$ reducible contribution.

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\subsection{Orbital decomposition of the internal propagation}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

We resolve the internal non-interacting two-particle propagator as
\begin{equation}
    L_0^\eh
    =
    L_0^{\eh,\mathrm{ov}}
    +
    L_0^{\eh,\mathrm{vo}}
    +
    L_0^{\eh,\mathrm{oo}}
    +
    L_0^{\eh,\mathrm{vv}}
    \label{eq:L0_sector_decomposition}
\end{equation}
where $ov$ contains the $ia$ sector associated with $Q^+$, $vo$ contains the
$ai$ sector associated with $Q^-$, and $oo$ and $vv$ contain $ij$ and $ab$,
respectively.

For an instantaneous interaction, the isolated $oo$ and $vv$ frequency
integrals vanish,
\begin{equation}
    \int\frac{\dd{x}}{2\pi}
    G_{0,i}(x+\omega)G_{0,j}(x)
    =
    0
    \qquad
    \int\frac{\dd{x}}{2\pi}
    G_{0,a}(x+\omega)G_{0,b}(x)
    =
    0
    \label{eq:same_occupancy_bubbles_zero}
\end{equation}
because the two one-particle poles lie in the same half-plane.
This is why only the $ov$ and $vo$ sectors contribute at second order.
At third order, however, $\Gamma^{\eh,(2)}$ supplies an additional pole and
the $oo$ and $vv$ sectors can survive.
For example, with $a,b,c$ virtual and $k$ occupied,
\begin{equation}
    \begin{split}
    &\int\frac{\dd{x}}{2\pi}
    \frac{1}{x+\omega-\eps_a+\ii0^+}
    \frac{1}{x-\eps_b+\ii0^+}
    \frac{1}{x-\nu+(\eps_c-\eps_k)-\ii0^+}
    \\
    &\qquad=
    \frac{\ii}
    {[\nu+\omega-(\eps_a+\eps_c-\eps_k)+\ii0^+]
     [\nu-(\eps_b+\eps_c-\eps_k)+\ii0^+]}
    \end{split}
    \label{eq:vv_dynamic_counterexample}
\end{equation}
The corresponding $oo$ integral is nonzero when the second-order kernel
provides a pole in the opposite half-plane.

The third term of Eq.~\eqref{eq:Phi_eh_third_convolution} contains only
instantaneous vertices.
Its $oo$ and $vv$ integrations therefore vanish, so both internal
propagations in that term belong to the $ov/vo$ sectors.

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\subsection{$ov$ and $vo$ sectors}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

The $ov$ and $vo$ parts of the first two terms in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:Phi_eh_third_convolution} can be reorganized as corrections to
the coupling vertices.
For the $ov$ sector, the left correction is
\begin{equation}
    U^{\eh,+,(2)}_{pr,ia}(\nu,\omega)
    =
    \frac{1}{2\pi}
    \int\dd{\bar\nu}
    \left[
    \ii\Gamma^{\eh,(2)}_{pa\,ri}(\nu,\bar\nu,\omega)
    \right]
    Q^+_{ia}(\bar\nu,\omega)
    \label{eq:U_eh_second_def}
\end{equation}
which gives
\begin{equation}
    \begin{split}
    U^{\eh,+,(2)}_{pr,ia}(\nu,\omega)
    & =
    \sum_{jb}
    \frac{\mel{pb}{}{ij}\mel{aj}{}{rb}}
    {\nu-(\eps_i+\eps_j-\eps_b)-\ii0^+}
    \\
    &-
    \sum_{jb}
    \frac{\mel{pj}{}{ib}\mel{ab}{}{rj}}
    {\nu+\omega-(\eps_a+\eps_b-\eps_j)+\ii0^+}
    \\
    &-
    \frac12
    \sum_{jk}
    \frac{\mel{pa}{}{jk}\mel{jk}{}{ri}}
    {\nu-(\eps_j+\eps_k-\eps_a)-\ii0^+}
    \\
    &+
    \frac12
    \sum_{bc}
    \frac{\mel{pa}{}{bc}\mel{bc}{}{ri}}
    {\nu+\omega-(\eps_b+\eps_c-\eps_i)+\ii0^+}
    \end{split}
    \label{eq:U_eh_second_explicit}
\end{equation}
The corresponding right correction is
\begin{equation}
    V^{\eh,+,(2)}_{ia,sq}(\nu',\omega)
    =
    \frac{1}{2\pi}
    \int\dd{\bar\nu}
    Q^+_{ia}(\bar\nu,\omega)
    \left[
    \ii\Gamma^{\eh,(2)}_{iq\,as}(\bar\nu,\nu',\omega)
    \right]
    \label{eq:V_eh_second_def}
\end{equation}
with
\begin{equation}
    \begin{split}
    V^{\eh,+,(2)}_{ia,sq}(\nu',\omega)
    & =
    \sum_{kc}
    \frac{\mel{sc}{}{ik}\mel{ak}{}{qc}}
    {\nu'-(\eps_i+\eps_k-\eps_c)-\ii0^+}
    \\
    &-
    \sum_{kc}
    \frac{\mel{sk}{}{ic}\mel{ac}{}{qk}}
    {\nu'+\omega-(\eps_a+\eps_c-\eps_k)+\ii0^+}
    \\
    &-
    \frac12
    \sum_{kl}
    \frac{\mel{sa}{}{kl}\mel{kl}{}{qi}}
    {\nu'-(\eps_k+\eps_l-\eps_a)-\ii0^+}
    \\
    &+
    \frac12
    \sum_{cd}
    \frac{\mel{sa}{}{cd}\mel{cd}{}{qi}}
    {\nu'+\omega-(\eps_c+\eps_d-\eps_i)+\ii0^+}
    \end{split}
    \label{eq:V_eh_second_explicit}
\end{equation}

For the $vo$ sector,
\begin{equation}
    U^{\eh,-,(2)}_{pr,ia}(\nu,\omega)
    =
    \frac{1}{2\pi}
    \int\dd{\bar\nu}
    \left[
    \ii\Gamma^{\eh,(2)}_{pi\,ra}(\nu,\bar\nu,\omega)
    \right]
    Q^-_{ia}(\bar\nu,\omega)
    \label{eq:U_eh_second_minus_def}
\end{equation}
and the contour integrations give
\begin{equation}
    \begin{split}
    U^{\eh,-,(2)}_{pr,ia}(\nu,\omega)
    & =
    \sum_{kc}
    \frac{\mel{pc}{}{ak}\mel{ik}{}{rc}}
    {\nu+\omega-(\eps_i+\eps_k-\eps_c)-\ii0^+}
    \\
    &-
    \sum_{kc}
    \frac{\mel{pk}{}{ac}\mel{ic}{}{rk}}
    {\nu-(\eps_a+\eps_c-\eps_k)+\ii0^+}
    \\
    &+
    \frac12
    \sum_{cd}
    \frac{\mel{pi}{}{cd}\mel{cd}{}{ra}}
    {\nu-(\eps_c+\eps_d-\eps_i)+\ii0^+}
    \\
    &-
    \frac12
    \sum_{kl}
    \frac{\mel{pi}{}{kl}\mel{kl}{}{ra}}
    {\nu+\omega-(\eps_k+\eps_l-\eps_a)-\ii0^+}
    \end{split}
    \label{eq:U_eh_second_minus_explicit}
\end{equation}
Likewise,
\begin{equation}
    V^{\eh,-,(2)}_{ia,sq}(\nu',\omega)
    =
    \frac{1}{2\pi}
    \int\dd{\bar\nu}
    Q^-_{ia}(\bar\nu,\omega)
    \left[
    \ii\Gamma^{\eh,(2)}_{aq\,is}(\bar\nu,\nu',\omega)
    \right]
    \label{eq:V_eh_second_minus_def}
\end{equation}
with
\begin{equation}
    \begin{split}
    V^{\eh,-,(2)}_{ia,sq}(\nu',\omega)
    & =
    \sum_{kc}
    \frac{\mel{ak}{}{sc}\mel{qc}{}{ik}}
    {\nu'+\omega-(\eps_i+\eps_k-\eps_c)-\ii0^+}
    \\
    &-
    \sum_{kc}
    \frac{\mel{ac}{}{sk}\mel{qk}{}{ic}}
    {\nu'-(\eps_a+\eps_c-\eps_k)+\ii0^+}
    \\
    &-
    \frac12
    \sum_{kl}
    \frac{\mel{aq}{}{kl}\mel{kl}{}{is}}
    {\nu'+\omega-(\eps_k+\eps_l-\eps_a)-\ii0^+}
    \\
    &+
    \frac12
    \sum_{cd}
    \frac{\mel{aq}{}{cd}\mel{cd}{}{is}}
    {\nu'-(\eps_c+\eps_d-\eps_i)+\ii0^+}
    \end{split}
    \label{eq:V_eh_second_minus_explicit}
\end{equation}
The $+$ and $-$ components contain only the same $\hhp$ and $\pph$ pole
families identified at second order.

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\subsection{$oo$ and $vv$ sectors}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

The $vv$ sector selects the poles of $\Gamma^{\eh,(2)}$ lying in the
half-plane opposite to the two virtual one-particle poles.
For the first ordering in Eq.~\eqref{eq:Phi_eh_third_convolution},
\begin{equation}
    \begin{split}
    &\left\{
    \left[\ii\Gamma^{\eh,(2)}\right]
    \circ\left[-\ii L_0^{\eh,\mathrm{vv}}\right]
    \circ\left[\ii\Gamma^{\eh,(1)}\right]
    \right\}_{pqrs}
    \\
    & =
    \sum_{abkc}
    \mel{pk}{}{bc}\mel{ac}{}{rk}\mel{qb}{}{sa}
    \\
    &\times
    \frac{1}{\nu+\omega-(\eps_a+\eps_c-\eps_k)+\ii0^+}
    \frac{1}{\nu-(\eps_b+\eps_c-\eps_k)+\ii0^+}
    \\
    &-
    \frac12
    \sum_{abkl}
    \mel{pa}{}{kl}\mel{kl}{}{rb}\mel{qb}{}{sa}
    \\
    &\times
    \frac{1}{\nu-(\eps_k+\eps_l-\eps_a)-\ii0^+}
    \frac{1}{\nu+\omega-(\eps_k+\eps_l-\eps_b)-\ii0^+}
    \end{split}
    \label{eq:Phi_eh_third_vv_left}
\end{equation}
and the opposite ordering gives
\begin{equation}
    \begin{split}
    &\left\{
    \left[\ii\Gamma^{\eh,(1)}\right]
    \circ\left[-\ii L_0^{\eh,\mathrm{vv}}\right]
    \circ\left[\ii\Gamma^{\eh,(2)}\right]
    \right\}_{pqrs}
    \\
    & =
    \sum_{abkc}
    \mel{pa}{}{rb}\mel{bc}{}{sk}\mel{qk}{}{ac}
    \\
    &\times
    \frac{1}{\nu'+\omega-(\eps_a+\eps_c-\eps_k)+\ii0^+}
    \frac{1}{\nu'-(\eps_b+\eps_c-\eps_k)+\ii0^+}
    \\
    &-
    \frac12
    \sum_{abkl}
    \mel{pa}{}{rb}\mel{bq}{}{kl}\mel{kl}{}{as}
    \\
    &\times
    \frac{1}{\nu'-(\eps_k+\eps_l-\eps_a)-\ii0^+}
    \frac{1}{\nu'+\omega-(\eps_k+\eps_l-\eps_b)-\ii0^+}
    \end{split}
    \label{eq:Phi_eh_third_vv_right}
\end{equation}

The $oo$ sector selects the opposite causal poles of the second-order kernel.
For the first ordering,
\begin{equation}
    \begin{split}
    &\left\{
    \left[\ii\Gamma^{\eh,(2)}\right]
    \circ\left[-\ii L_0^{\eh,\mathrm{oo}}\right]
    \circ\left[\ii\Gamma^{\eh,(1)}\right]
    \right\}_{pqrs}
    \\
    & =
    \sum_{ijkc}
    \mel{pc}{}{jk}\mel{ik}{}{rc}\mel{qj}{}{si}
    \\
    &\times
    \frac{1}{\nu+\omega-(\eps_i+\eps_k-\eps_c)-\ii0^+}
    \frac{1}{\nu-(\eps_j+\eps_k-\eps_c)-\ii0^+}
    \\
    &-
    \frac12
    \sum_{ijcd}
    \mel{pi}{}{cd}\mel{cd}{}{rj}\mel{qj}{}{si}
    \\
    &\times
    \frac{1}{\nu-(\eps_c+\eps_d-\eps_i)+\ii0^+}
    \frac{1}{\nu+\omega-(\eps_c+\eps_d-\eps_j)+\ii0^+}
    \end{split}
    \label{eq:Phi_eh_third_oo_left}
\end{equation}
and for the opposite ordering,
\begin{equation}
    \begin{split}
    &\left\{
    \left[\ii\Gamma^{\eh,(1)}\right]
    \circ\left[-\ii L_0^{\eh,\mathrm{oo}}\right]
    \circ\left[\ii\Gamma^{\eh,(2)}\right]
    \right\}_{pqrs}
    \\
    & =
    \sum_{ijkc}
    \mel{pi}{}{rj}\mel{jk}{}{sc}\mel{qc}{}{ik}
    \\
    &\times
    \frac{1}{\nu'+\omega-(\eps_i+\eps_k-\eps_c)-\ii0^+}
    \frac{1}{\nu'-(\eps_j+\eps_k-\eps_c)-\ii0^+}
    \\
    &-
    \frac12
    \sum_{ijcd}
    \mel{pi}{}{rj}\mel{jq}{}{cd}\mel{cd}{}{is}
    \\
    &\times
    \frac{1}{\nu'-(\eps_c+\eps_d-\eps_i)+\ii0^+}
    \frac{1}{\nu'+\omega-(\eps_c+\eps_d-\eps_j)+\ii0^+}
    \end{split}
    \label{eq:Phi_eh_third_oo_right}
\end{equation}
These expressions are deliberately left as products of charged-state
resolvents.
No partial-fraction decomposition is introduced, so no denominator involving
$\eps_a-\eps_b$ or $\eps_i-\eps_j$ appears.

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\subsection{Three-interaction term}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

Because all three vertices in the last term of
Eq.~\eqref{eq:Phi_eh_third_convolution} are instantaneous, only the $ov$ and
$vo$ sectors survive the internal frequency integrations.
The first-order neutral interaction matrix is
\begin{equation}
    M^{\eh,(1)}
    =
    \begin{pmatrix}
    A^{(1)}&B^{(1)}
    \\
    B^{(1)\intercal}&A^{(1)\intercal}
    \end{pmatrix}
    \label{eq:M_eh_first}
\end{equation}
with
\begin{align}
    A^{(1)}_{ia,jb}
    &=
    \mel{aj}{}{ib}
    \\
    B^{(1)}_{ia,jb}
    &=
    \mel{ab}{}{ij}
\end{align}
and
\begin{equation}
    R^{\eh,(1)}
    =
    R^{\eh,(0)}M^{\eh,(1)}R^{\eh,(0)}
    \label{eq:R_eh_first}
\end{equation}
Thus the complete contribution from the three instantaneous interactions is
\begin{equation}
    U^{\eh,(1)}R^{\eh,(0)}M^{\eh,(1)}R^{\eh,(0)}V^{\eh,(1)}
    \label{eq:Phi_eh_third_three_bare}
\end{equation}
For example, its forward-forward block is
\begin{equation}
    \begin{split}
    &\left[
    U^{\eh,(1)}R^{\eh,(0)}M^{\eh,(1)}R^{\eh,(0)}V^{\eh,(1)}
    \right]^{++}_{pqrs}
    \\
    &\quad=
    \sum_{ia,jb}
    \frac{
    \mel{pa}{}{ri}\mel{aj}{}{ib}\mel{qj}{}{sb}
    }
    {[\omega-(\eps_a-\eps_i)+\ii0^+]
     [\omega-(\eps_b-\eps_j)+\ii0^+]}
    \end{split}
    \label{eq:Phi_eh_third_three_bare_pp}
\end{equation}

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\subsection{Complete direct eh result through third order}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

The complete result is most transparently written by retaining the four
orbital sectors of Eq.~\eqref{eq:L0_sector_decomposition},
\begin{equation}
    \begin{split}
    \ii\Phi^{\eh,(3)}
    ={}&
    \sum_{x\in\{\mathrm{ov},\mathrm{vo},\mathrm{oo},\mathrm{vv}\}}
    \Big(
    \left[\ii\Gamma^{\eh,(2)}\right]
    \circ\left[-\ii L_0^{\eh,x}\right]
    \circ\left[\ii\Gamma^{\eh,(1)}\right]
    \\
    &\qquad+
    \left[\ii\Gamma^{\eh,(1)}\right]
    \circ\left[-\ii L_0^{\eh,x}\right]
    \circ\left[\ii\Gamma^{\eh,(2)}\right]
    \Big)
    \\
    &+
    U^{\eh,(1)}R^{\eh,(0)}M^{\eh,(1)}R^{\eh,(0)}V^{\eh,(1)}
    \end{split}
    \label{eq:Phi_eh_third_final}
\end{equation}
The $ov$ and $vo$ subset of the first two lines is equivalently
\begin{equation}
    U^{\eh,(2)}R^{\eh,(0)}V^{\eh,(1)}
    +
    U^{\eh,(1)}R^{\eh,(0)}V^{\eh,(2)}
    \label{eq:Phi_eh_third_ovvo_resolvent}
\end{equation}
while the $oo$ and $vv$ terms are given explicitly in
Eqs.~\eqref{eq:Phi_eh_third_vv_left}--\eqref{eq:Phi_eh_third_oo_right}.
The three-interaction term is Eq.~\eqref{eq:Phi_eh_third_three_bare} and is
equivalent to $U^{\eh,(1)}R^{\eh,(1)}V^{\eh,(1)}$.
No additional orbital sector or third-order topology occurs in the direct
$\eh$ reducible vertex.

\section{pp vertex through third order}
\label{sec:pp_third}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

The $\pp$ construction will be completed after the direct-$\eh$ expressions
have been audited.
For the moment, we retain only the part that follows immediately from the
second-order multiresolvent representation.
The first-order correction to the central $\pp$ matrix is
\begin{equation}
    M^{\pp,(1)}
    =
    \begin{pmatrix}
    \mel{ab}{}{cd}&\mel{ab}{}{ij}
    \\
    \mel{ij}{}{cd}&\mel{ij}{}{kl}
    \end{pmatrix}
    \label{eq:M_pp_first}
\end{equation}
and
\begin{equation}
    R^{\pp,(1)}
    =
    R^{\pp,(0)}M^{\pp,(1)}R^{\pp,(0)}
    \label{eq:R_pp_first}
\end{equation}
The second-order coupling-vertex corrections $U^{\pp,(2)}$ and
$V^{\pp,(2)}$ are generated by
$\Gamma^{\pp,(2)}=\Phi^{\eh,(2)}+\Phi^{\teh,(2)}$ and retain the same
$(N-1)$ $\hhp$ and $(N+1)$ $\pph$ pole families as in the direct-$\eh$
construction.
The $\ee/\hh$ part therefore contains
\begin{equation}
    U^{\pp,(2)}R^{\pp,(0)}V^{\pp,(1)}
    +
    U^{\pp,(1)}R^{\pp,(0)}V^{\pp,(2)}
    +
    U^{\pp,(1)}R^{\pp,(1)}V^{\pp,(1)}
    \label{eq:Phi_pp_third_eehh}
\end{equation}
For example, the pure $\ee$ three-interaction contribution is
\begin{equation}
    \begin{split}
    &\left[
    U^{\pp,(1)}R^{\pp,(0)}M^{\pp,(1)}R^{\pp,(0)}V^{\pp,(1)}
    \right]_{pqrs}^{\ee}
    \\
    &\quad=
    \sum_{\substack{a<b\\c<d}}
    \frac{
    \mel{pq}{}{ab}\mel{ab}{}{cd}\mel{cd}{}{rs}
    }
    {[\Omega_\pp-(\eps_a+\eps_b)+\ii0^+]
     [\Omega_\pp-(\eps_c+\eps_d)+\ii0^+]}
    \end{split}
    \label{eq:Phi_pp_third_central_ee}
\end{equation}
A complete $\pp$ derivation must still resolve the internal two-particle
propagation by orbital sector and evaluate the sectors that become nonzero
when $\Gamma^{\pp,(2)}$ is frequency dependent.
Those terms are intentionally not introduced here before their frequency
routing and antisymmetric-pair normalization have been derived explicitly.
In the direct-$\eh$ frequency convention,
$\Omega_\pp=\omega+\nu+\nu'$.

\section{Consistency checks at third order}
\label{sec:checks_third}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

The direct-$\eh$ formulas admit several independent checks.
These checks will be applied in the final audit of
Sec.~\ref{sec:eh_third}; the second-order validations are not repeated here.

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\subsection{Orbital-sector completeness}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

Equation~\eqref{eq:L0_sector_decomposition} is an identity at the level of
the internal orbital sum.
Consequently, the first two terms of
Eq.~\eqref{eq:Phi_eh_third_convolution} are complete only when all four
$ov$, $vo$, $oo$, and $vv$ sectors have been evaluated.
The $ov/vo$ subset is represented compactly by
Eq.~\eqref{eq:Phi_eh_third_ovvo_resolvent}, whereas the $oo/vv$ subset is
given directly by Eqs.~\eqref{eq:Phi_eh_third_vv_left}--
\eqref{eq:Phi_eh_third_oo_right}.
The last term of Eq.~\eqref{eq:Phi_eh_third_convolution} contains only
$ov/vo$ propagation because its vertices are instantaneous.

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\subsection{Pair reversal}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

The backward coupling vertices can be checked independently from
\begin{equation}
    Q^-_{ia}(\nu,\omega)
    =
    Q^+_{ia}(\nu+\omega,-\omega)
    \label{eq:Q_reversal_third}
\end{equation}
together with the corresponding orbital-index reversal.
This provides a direct check of the denominators and causal prescriptions in
Eqs.~\eqref{eq:U_eh_second_minus_explicit} and
\eqref{eq:V_eh_second_minus_explicit}.

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\subsection{Crossing}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

The transverse $\eh$ contribution is generated from the direct channel by
fermionic crossing.
At every perturbative order,
\begin{equation}
    \Phi^{\teh,(n)}_{pqrs}(\nu,\nu',\omega)
    =
    -
    \Phi^{\eh,(n)}_{pqsr}
    \left(
    \nu,
    \nu+\omega,
    \nu'-\nu
    \right)
    \label{eq:crossing_order_n}
\end{equation}
The transformation must be applied to the complete direct-$\eh$ expression,
including the $oo$ and $vv$ sectors.

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\subsection{Parquet reconstruction}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

Because the fully irreducible vertex has no third-order correction,
\begin{align}
    F^{(3)}
    &=
    \Phi^{\eh,(3)}+\Phi^{\teh,(3)}+\Phi^{\pp,(3)}
    \\
    \Gamma^{\eh,(3)}
    &=
    \Phi^{\teh,(3)}+\Phi^{\pp,(3)}
    \\
    \Gamma^{\pp,(3)}
    &=
    \Phi^{\eh,(3)}+\Phi^{\teh,(3)}
    \label{eq:parquet_third_reconstruction}
\end{align}

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\subsection{High-frequency structure}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

The second-order coupling-vertex corrections decay as
\begin{equation}
    U^{(2)}
    =
    \mathcal O(\nu^{-1})
    \qquad
    V^{(2)}
    =
    \mathcal O({\nu'}^{-1})
    \label{eq:coupling_high_frequency}
\end{equation}
The explicit $oo$ and $vv$ expressions provide an additional check: each is
a product of two charged-state denominators and therefore has the expected
large-fermionic-frequency decay.
No partial-fraction representation involving $\eps_a-\eps_b$ or
$\eps_i-\eps_j$ is required for this check.

\section{Recipe through third order}
\label{sec:recipe}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

For the direct-$\eh$ vertex, begin from the three BSE contributions in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:Phi_eh_third_convolution} and decompose every internal
$L_0^\eh$ according to Eq.~\eqref{eq:L0_sector_decomposition}.
Evaluate the $ov$ and $vo$ sectors with $Q^+$ and $Q^-$, respectively.
They generate the second-order coupling vertices
$U^{\eh,\pm,(2)}$ and $V^{\eh,\pm,(2)}$ and can be reorganized as
Eq.~\eqref{eq:Phi_eh_third_ovvo_resolvent}.

Evaluate the $oo$ and $vv$ sectors directly in the frequency convolution.
Because $\Gamma^{\eh,(2)}$ is frequency dependent, these contributions do
not vanish and are given by
Eqs.~\eqref{eq:Phi_eh_third_vv_left}--\eqref{eq:Phi_eh_third_oo_right}.
Keep the resulting products of charged-state resolvents in that form rather
than introducing differences between two occupied or two virtual orbital
energies.

Finally, evaluate the three-interaction term.
Only $ov/vo$ propagation survives and the result is
Eq.~\eqref{eq:Phi_eh_third_three_bare}.
Combining all sectors gives Eq.~\eqref{eq:Phi_eh_third_final}.
After the direct-$\eh$ formulas have passed the sign, index-routing,
pair-reversal, and crossing audit, the same sector-by-sector strategy can be
applied to the $\pp$ channel.
